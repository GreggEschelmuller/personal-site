Hi, I’m Gregg.

I’m a researcher with a background in human movement and motor control. I recently finished a PhD at UBC studying how the brain uses sensory feedback to control movement.

I’m also interested in programming, statistics, machine learning, and scientific computing. I like working with data, building models, and more recently I have really enjoyed learning to build research tools.

This site is where I keep my research, projects, and notes on things I’m learning. I plan to write about a fairly wide range of topics, mostly because I’ve found that trying to explain something in writing is a good way to discover what I don’t understand as well as I thought I did. Some posts will be more developed than others, and I’m sure I’ll get things wrong along the way.

If you find an error, please let me know at g.eschelmuller@gmail.com .
