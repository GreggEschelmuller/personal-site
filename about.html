About

A bit about me.

I study human movement, teach physiology and motor behavior, and enjoy working on data science problems.

Profile

I completed a PhD in Kinesiology focused on motor control and proprioception. Most of my research asks how people use sensory feedback to move accurately, especially when that feedback is noisy or misleading.

I build and run human experiments, working with both experimental and computational methods. I am primarily interested in using Bayesian approaches to understand how the brain solves motor control problems.

Current focus

  • Proprioception, tendon vibration, and sensorimotor adaptation.
  • Teaching physiology, motor behavior, and movement science.
  • Experiment code, signal processing, modeling, and reproducible workflows.
