About

I’m based in Vancouver, and my background is in kinesiology and motor control. I finished my PhD at the University of British Columbia in 2026.

Most of my academic work involved designing experiments to understand proprioception: the sense that tells us where our limbs are without having to look at them. I did this through a mix of human behavioural, neurophysiological, or computational experiments/methods.

The computational parts of research became interests of their own. I really enjoy programming, working through statistical models, and making analysis workflows easier to understand and reuse. Lately I’ve been spending more time on machine learning and building scientific software.

I’m still interested in movement science, alongside the programming and data work I’ve been doing more recently.

Elsewhere on the site you can find my research, projects, writing, and CV.
